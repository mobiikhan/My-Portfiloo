Introduction

A Little About Me

I am a Software Engineering graduate with a focused dedication to UI/UX design and frontend web development.

My passion lies in bridging the gap between human needs and digital solutions. I enjoy understanding user requirements, mapping out clear user journeys, crafting low-to-high fidelity wireframes, and building fully interactive prototypes in Figma. Once the design is perfected, I bring interfaces to life using semantic HTML5, modern CSS3, and JavaScript—ensuring every interaction is responsive, intuitive, and accessible.

Through my freelance client work and multiple internships, I have collaborated with cross-functional teams, incorporated constructive design feedback through iterative sprints, and practiced continuous learning to stay ahead with modern web trends and AI-assisted design workflows.

Methodology

My Design Process

A structured, user-centric workflow from initial requirements to refined interactive interfaces.

01

Understand Requirements

Deconstruct the project scope, identify target users, business objectives, and define technical boundaries.

02

Research & User Needs

Investigate user pain points, analyze comparable solutions, and establish fundamental user personas.

03

Wireframes & User Flows

Map out information architecture, frictionless journeys, and structural low-fidelity screen layouts.

04

High-Fidelity UI in Figma

Design pixel-perfect interfaces with harmonious color systems, typography tokens, and interactive prototypes.

05

Build Responsive Frontend

Translate UI designs into performant, clean HTML5, CSS3, and JavaScript adhering to responsive standards.

06

Review, Test & Refine

Inspect multi-device usability, validate contrast and responsiveness, and iterate based on stakeholder feedback.
